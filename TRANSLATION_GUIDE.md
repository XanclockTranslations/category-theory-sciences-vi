# Translation and fidelity conventions

- Translate the supplied 2014 edition, not an older 2013 course draft or another
  online edition. Online sources may help recover notation but do not change
  the authoritative text.
- Preserve every definition, hypothesis, theorem, proof, worked example,
  exercise, answer, footnote, figure/table, citation, and reference. Translate
  mathematical meaning precisely rather than summarize it.
- Preserve original section, example, equation, exercise, and figure numbers.
  Add `% Source: printed p. N; PDF p. N+9` comments at source page boundaries.
- Diagrams are mathematical content: redraw their topology and translate their
  labels faithfully. Keep the source crop available for comparison. Do not
  silently leave a missing diagram as an empty placeholder in a delivered PDF.
- Use fluent formal Vietnamese, not word-by-word English syntax. Preserve
  degrees of certainty and the distinction between the author's claims and
  established results. Keep names, identifiers, and mathematical symbols intact.
- Use the shared glossary. Initially: category = phạm trù; functor = hàm tử;
  natural transformation = biến đổi tự nhiên; morphism = cấu xạ; object = đối
  tượng; set = tập hợp; function = hàm; monoid = monoid; group = nhóm;
  adjunction = liên hợp; limit = giới hạn; colimit = đối giới hạn; product = tích;
  coproduct = đối tích; schema = lược đồ; instance = thể hiện; olog = olog
  (nhật ký bản thể); sheaf = bó; monad = monad; operad = operad. These working
  choices can be revised globally after reviewing established Vietnamese usage.
  Retain the borrowed term topos (plural toposes in English), distinguished
  from tôpô (topology).
- Use tôpô consistently in the body and index. Use nghịch biến (contravariant)
  consistently; phản biến is a synonym, not a different kind of functor.
  Distinguish trình bày (presentation by generators and relations) from
  biểu diễn (representation). Retain cobordism with its explanatory definition
  rather than use đối biên, which risks confusion with coboundary.
- Further coordinated terms: pullback = kéo lui; pushout = đẩy ra;
  fiber product = tích thớ; fiber sum = tổng thớ; equalizer = bộ cân bằng;
  coequalizer = đối bộ cân bằng; data migration = di trú dữ liệu;
  whiskering = ghép bên; adjunct = cấu xạ tương ứng qua liên hợp.
  Keep span, cospan, monoid, monad, operad and topos as borrowed terms,
  explaining them at first definition.
- Indiscrete = thô, introduced as “thô (indiscrete)” at the relevant first
  definition of a graph/preorder/category. This names the specific maximal
  relation or unique-arrow construction, rather than arbitrary non-discreteness.
  Keep the functor identifier Ind unchanged.
- Keep explicit issues in review notes. Do not mark a draft independently
  reviewed until another reviewer has compared it to the relevant source pages.
- For a visually verified source typo with an unambiguous mathematical
  correction, use a clearly identified translator footnote preserving the
  original expression and explaining the correction. Record it in the review.
  Preserve ambiguous source discrepancies and flag them, rather than guess.
- Bibliographic titles remain in their original language. Index terms are
  translated and repaginated for the Vietnamese edition.
- Deliver a verified PDF and editable sources. Record partial progress honestly;
  previews are not completion of the whole book.
